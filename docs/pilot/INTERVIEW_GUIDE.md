# Campus mess and NGO interview guide

Status: prepared questions; no interviews or quotes collected.

## Before the conversation

Explain the student project, expected 15-minute duration, voluntary participation,
and that declining questions will not affect access to the service. Obtain separate
permission to take notes, record audio, quote words, name the organisation and use
material in the course report. Use participant codes M01 and N01. Keep contact
information and raw identifiable notes outside Git. Do not contact anyone or run a
pilot without the user's authorization.

## Campus mess / donor

1. Describe the last occasion when edible surplus was left at the end of service.
2. When is quantity known? How much time remains for a collection?
3. What storage, packaging and ingredient/allergen information can staff provide?
4. How is surplus handled today, and where do collections fail?
5. Who can approve a donation and confirm the actual quantity at 8 pm?
6. Which alerts reach staff reliably? Is a daily reminder useful?
7. What would prevent one bounded trial collection?

## NGO / receiver

1. Describe the last surplus collection you accepted or declined.
2. What quantity, dietary needs, ingredient information and handling details matter?
3. Who confirms collection, and how are volunteers assigned?
4. What is a realistic response time after an alert?
5. How do missed pickups and changed quantities get resolved today?
6. Which channel works on staff phones? Where is mobile coverage unreliable?
7. What evidence would make you trust one trial collection?
